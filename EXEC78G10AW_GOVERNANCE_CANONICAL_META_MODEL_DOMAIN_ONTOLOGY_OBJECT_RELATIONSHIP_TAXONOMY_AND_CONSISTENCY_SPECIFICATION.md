The decisive rule is:

```text
governance meta-model
  != new governance concept

  != authority modification
  != register modification
  != SoR modification
  != readiness modification
  != evaluation modification
  != decision modification
  != validation
  != ontology evaluation

  != meta-model evaluation
  != truth determination
  != validity establishment
  != readiness determination
  != authorization
  != operational effect
  != active reliance
```

A governance ontology is a consolidated descriptive hierarchy of previously defined governance concepts.

A governance taxonomy is a classification of previously defined domains, objects, relationships, dependencies, lifecycles, authorities, evidence structures, evaluations, and decisions.

A canonical governance meta-model is a descriptive architectural assembly of those previously defined concepts and taxonomies.

Ontology representation shall not constitute validation.

Taxonomy representation shall not constitute evaluation.

Meta-model representation shall not constitute execution.

Ontology, taxonomy, and meta-model structures shall not establish truth, validity, readiness, authorization, operational effect, or active reliance.

This phase audits and consolidates G.10AN through G.10AV. It does not supersede, amend, replace, or operationalize those contracts.
