# AI Upsell and Pricing

Pricing now presents OpenStaff plans by contact limits and RELU AI capability depth.

## Plans

- BASIC: public discovery, limited contact access, manual review
- BRONZE: RELU compatibility prompts, project intake parsing, moderated media previews
- GOLD: advanced parsing, predictive risk summaries, priority contractor matching
- ENTERPRISE: custom RELU workflows, auto-approve policy controls, dedicated procurement support

## Upsell Triggers

Contextual upgrade prompts apply when:

- private contact limits are reached
- RELU detects high compatibility
- advanced parsing is needed
- predictive matching/risk summaries are gated by plan

Example: `RELU AI detected 95% compatibility with this subcontractor. Upgrade to unlock predictive outreach.`
