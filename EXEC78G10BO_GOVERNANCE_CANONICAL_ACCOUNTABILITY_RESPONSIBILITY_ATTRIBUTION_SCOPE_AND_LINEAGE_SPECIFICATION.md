## WP G10BO-A Canonical Accountability Architecture

### A.1 Accountability Classes

Canonical accountability structures may classify an accountability reference as:

- `ACCOUNTABILITY_REFERENCE`: a descriptive pointer that an object, actor, role, authority, control, policy, decision, event, or lifecycle state is associated with an accountability concept.
- `ANSWERABILITY_REFERENCE`: a descriptive pointer that a future process may ask for explanation, response, or evidence from a referenced party or structure.
- `STEWARDSHIP_REFERENCE`: a descriptive pointer to maintenance, care, custody, preservation, or oversight interest.
- `OWNERSHIP_REFERENCE`: a descriptive pointer to ownership-of-record or ownership-of-context, without establishing legal ownership, operational control, or liability.
- `RESPONSIBILITY_REFERENCE`: a descriptive pointer to a possible responsibility structure, without assigning responsibility.
- `ATTRIBUTION_REFERENCE`: a descriptive pointer to source, contribution, cause-candidate, influence, actor, process, input, or event relationship, without determining fault.
- `ACCOUNTABILITY_BOUNDARY_REFERENCE`: a descriptive pointer to the boundary inside which accountability concepts may be represented.
- `ACCOUNTABILITY_LINEAGE_REFERENCE`: a descriptive pointer to predecessor, successor, delegation, replay, or reconstruction lineage.
